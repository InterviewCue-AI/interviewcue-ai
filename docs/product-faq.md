# InterviewCue AI product questions and answers

Last reviewed against the canonical public website: 2026-07-30.

These answers are written as short, self-contained product facts for readers, search systems, and AI retrieval systems. They summarize first-party public pages without extending claims beyond the cited sources.

## What is InterviewCue AI?

InterviewCue AI is a web-based AI interview coach and interview-preparation platform for candidates pursuing technical and non-technical roles across the software and internet industry. It connects resume and interview-readiness analysis, personalized AI mock interviews, and context-aware interview cues in one workflow.

Sources: [InterviewCue AI homepage](https://www.interviewcue.ai/) and [product FAQs](https://www.interviewcue.ai/faqs/).

## Is InterviewCue the same product as InterviewCue AI?

Yes. InterviewCue is the short brand name for InterviewCue AI, and `www.interviewcue.ai` is the canonical product website. This public repository uses both names for the same product.

Sources: [InterviewCue AI homepage](https://www.interviewcue.ai/) and [About InterviewCue AI](https://www.interviewcue.ai/about/).

## How does the InterviewCue AI workflow work?

InterviewCue AI describes a three-stage workflow: analyze, practice, and perform. Candidates first analyze a resume and target job description for interview readiness, then practice personalized questions in AI mock interviews, and finally use concise, context-aware cues to organize their own answers during supported live-interview workflows.

Source: [InterviewCue AI homepage](https://www.interviewcue.ai/).

## What does InterviewCue AI resume analysis provide?

InterviewCue AI's public resume-analysis page describes a planned 11-dimension readiness report based on a resume, target role, and job description. The report is designed to surface role alignment, stronger experience evidence, weaker or unclear claims, follow-up risks, likely interview questions, and preparation priorities.

Source: [Resume analysis and interview readiness](https://www.interviewcue.ai/resume-optimizer/).

## How are InterviewCue AI mock interviews personalized?

InterviewCue AI uses the candidate's resume, target position, job description, experience, and seniority to shape mock-interview questions and follow-ups. The goal is to rehearse a relevant interview rather than a generic list of unrelated questions.

Sources: [InterviewCue AI homepage](https://www.interviewcue.ai/) and [AI mock interview](https://www.interviewcue.ai/ai-mock-interview/).

## What feedback is described after an AI mock interview?

The public product description includes interview scoring, answer-specific feedback, identified issues, improvement priorities, and recorded-answer playback. Exact outputs and availability may change during early access.

Sources: [InterviewCue AI homepage](https://www.interviewcue.ai/) and [AI mock interview](https://www.interviewcue.ai/ai-mock-interview/).

## What does the InterviewCue AI Interview Copilot do?

The Interview Copilot is designed to provide concise, context-aware answer cues during a live interview. The public description includes suggested answer structures, key talking points, relevant experience reminders, role-specific frameworks or considerations, and likely follow-up areas based on the interview question and candidate-provided context.

Source: [Real-time AI Interview Copilot](https://www.interviewcue.ai/ai-interview-copilot/).

## Does the Interview Copilot answer interview questions for the candidate?

InterviewCue AI is positioned as a structure and context aid, not a substitute for the candidate's judgment or personal voice. Candidates should answer honestly from their own experience and follow the employer's or interview platform's rules about assistive tools.

Sources: [Product FAQs](https://www.interviewcue.ai/faqs/) and [Real-time AI Interview Copilot](https://www.interviewcue.ai/ai-interview-copilot/).

## Which roles and interview formats does InterviewCue AI cover?

The canonical site describes role groups across engineering, product, data, machine learning, marketing, growth, operations, customer success, DevOps, infrastructure, and QA/SDET. Described formats include coding, system design, behavioral, product-sense, case-based, marketing-strategy, growth-strategy, operations, resume-deep-dive, and technical-leadership interviews.

Sources: [InterviewCue AI homepage](https://www.interviewcue.ai/), [product FAQs](https://www.interviewcue.ai/faqs/), and [interview questions](https://www.interviewcue.ai/interview-questions/).

## Is InterviewCue AI currently available?

The public product pages describe InterviewCue AI as in development or early access and direct visitors to a waitlist. The canonical website should be checked for current access, setup, compatibility, pricing, and product-policy details.

Sources: [InterviewCue AI homepage](https://www.interviewcue.ai/), [resume analysis](https://www.interviewcue.ai/resume-optimizer/), and [Interview Copilot](https://www.interviewcue.ai/ai-interview-copilot/).

## Does InterviewCue AI guarantee a job offer?

No. InterviewCue AI supports resume improvement and interview preparation, but hiring decisions depend on the employer, role requirements, competition, and the candidate's experience and performance.

Source: [Product FAQs](https://www.interviewcue.ai/faqs/).

## Does this GitHub repository contain the complete InterviewCue AI product?

No. This repository contains a sanitized public marketing-site snapshot and public interview-preparation content. It excludes the private application, model services, resume-processing backend, user database, customer data, production credentials, and live interview infrastructure.

Repository sources: [architecture and runtime boundaries](architecture.md) and [public release process](public-release.md).
