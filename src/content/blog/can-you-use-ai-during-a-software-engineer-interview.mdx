## Why the answer keeps changing

The answer keeps changing because employer policy is changing.

As of June 18, 2026, some employers are moving toward explicitly AI-aware interview formats. Google disclosed in a May 7, 2026 Business Insider report that it plans to pilot approved AI use in a code-comprehension round for some software engineering candidates. Meta also signaled in 2025 that it was exploring AI-enabled coding interviews. At the same time, other companies have warned candidates not to use outside AI support in coding or live interview rounds.

So the question is no longer "Is AI always banned?" or "Is AI always fine?" It is whether this company, this round, and this exact instruction set allow it.

## What is allowed when you use AI during a software engineer interview

What is allowed when you use AI during a software engineer interview usually depends on three things:

1. The employer's written rules.
2. The format of the round.
3. Whether the tool is explicitly approved.

In practice, the safest categories are:

- Practice before the interview.
- Resume or project reflection before the process begins.
- Approved tools named by the employer for a specific round.

The riskiest categories are:

- Real-time answer prompting in a round that has not explicitly allowed it.
- Hidden support during screen-shared coding or system design.
- Using outside tools when the interviewer expects unaided reasoning.

If you are unsure, ask the recruiter in writing. That one step matters more than any generic internet advice.

## Can you use AI during a software engineer interview vs AI mock interview

Can you use AI during a software engineer interview vs AI mock interview is the distinction most candidates should care about.

An AI mock interview is a practice environment. Its job is to help you sharpen explanations, rehearse tradeoffs, and get feedback before the real round. That is where tools like InterviewCue, live coding interview assistant practice, and software engineer interview questions with AI practice can be useful.

A live interview is an employer-controlled evaluation. If the company has not permitted AI, using real-time help can create authenticity and policy problems quickly.

That is why the most durable strategy is to use AI for rehearsal, not assumption. Build the skill during mock sessions so you can still perform if the real interview is fully unaided.

## Can you use AI during a software engineer interview for code comprehension rounds

Can you use AI during a software engineer interview for code comprehension rounds is where the rules are changing fastest.

Some employers now believe code comprehension with approved AI tools mirrors real work better than old whiteboard habits. Google's reported pilot is one example. In those cases, the skill being tested includes prompt quality, output verification, debugging judgment, and deciding when not to trust the model.

But the keyword there is approved. If the company says you may use a specific tool in a specific round, then the evaluation has changed by design. If the company does not say that, you should treat the round as closed-book.

Good prep for these rounds should focus on:

- Reading unfamiliar code quickly.
- Verifying AI suggestions instead of accepting them.
- Explaining edits and tradeoffs clearly.
- Catching subtle bugs or edge cases after AI output.

## Can you use AI during a software engineer interview review

A credible can you use AI during a software engineer interview review should not promise a universal yes or no.

Use this review framework instead:

- Was AI explicitly permitted?
- Was the permitted scope clear?
- Would the interviewer reasonably expect independent work?
- Could you still explain every decision without the tool?
- Would you be comfortable stating your usage out loud?

If the answer breaks down on those questions, the safer move is to stop and revert to your own reasoning. That is also why InterviewCue should be treated as a rehearsal product first. The better your practice, the less tempted you are to reach for unsafe support in the live round.
